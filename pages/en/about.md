My name is Linor Nezri-Sher, and I'm a speech-language therapist with **8 years of experience**. At my clinic in Moshav Kfar HaNagid, near Ness Ziona, Yavne and Rehovot, I support children and families on the way to more confident, fuller communication.

I hold an **M.A. in Early Childhood** from Hadassah Academic College. In my work I see the close connection between language, emotion and cognition, so every treatment is tailored personally – at the child's own pace, and in full partnership with parents and educational staff.

Alongside my clinical work, I lecture on early childhood and share knowledge and practical tools on my page [Bemachshava Mila](https://www.facebook.com/people/%D7%91%D7%9E%D7%97%D7%A9%D7%91%D7%94-%D7%9E%D7%99%D7%9C%D7%94/61584878556953/) – for speech therapists, educators and parents.

## Lectures

Talks for educational staff, therapists and parents on early childhood:

- Between policy and practice
- A shared language for educators and therapists
- The connection between language, emotion and cognition
