We consider it important that this website is accessible to people with disabilities, so that everyone can find information and get in touch easily.

## Accessibility level

The website was built following Israeli Standard 5568 for web content accessibility, which is based on WCAG 2.0 level AA.

The website has been checked with automated testing tools and manually. [TO COMPLETE: date of the manual review and who performed it]

## What has been made accessible

- Full keyboard navigation, including a "Skip to content" link and a clear focus outline
- Screen reader support: proper heading structure, text descriptions for images, and clear names for buttons
- Sufficient color contrast, and text that can be enlarged up to 200% without losing content
- Responsive layout for phones and screens of any size
- No continuously moving content; animations stop for users who enabled "reduce motion" on their device
- Content language is marked, and a full Hebrew version is available
- Screen readers announce links that open in a new tab

## Known limitations

- Links to external services (WhatsApp, Waze, Facebook) lead to websites outside our control; their accessibility is the responsibility of their operators.
- In the preview image shown when the link is shared, the text is part of the image.
