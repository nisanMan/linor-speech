## When should I see a speech-language therapist?

When you feel that language or speech isn't developing as expected, when your child is hard to understand, or when there are communication difficulties. It's always better to check early – even a single consultation can bring clarity.

## How long does therapy take?

It varies with the area and with each child's needs. After the assessment, we can estimate the expected scope of therapy.

## Can I get reimbursed?

In many cases a partial refund is available from your health fund (kupat holim) or supplementary insurance, depending on eligibility. I'm happy to explain in a call.

## Where do sessions take place?

At the clinic, 96 HaErez St., Moshav Kfar HaNagid – near Ness Ziona, Yavne and Rehovot.
