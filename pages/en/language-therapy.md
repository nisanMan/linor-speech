In therapy I combine up-to-date professional knowledge with play, creativity and attentive listening, and build a personal plan for each child that fits their abilities, pace and needs.

Working **hand in hand with parents** matters to me – so that progress continues at home, in preschool and at school.

## How to get started

- **Introductory call** – a short, no-obligation conversation to understand your needs and answer your questions.
- **Assessment** – a structured evaluation session, followed by a summary and recommendations.
- **Personal plan** – setting therapy goals together with the family.
- **Therapy and support** – regular sessions, progress tracking and ongoing guidance.
