Assessment and therapy in speech, language and communication, tailored to each child and each family.

- **Assessment and evaluation** – a comprehensive evaluation of language, speech and communication skills, ending with a clear summary and recommendations.
- **Language development** – late language development, limited vocabulary, difficulty understanding or forming sentences.
- **Articulation and speech** – sound errors, and improving the clarity and intelligibility of speech.
- **Communication and play** – developing conversation, play and interaction skills, with an understanding of the link between language and emotion.
- **Parent guidance** – simple, everyday tools to support language and communication at home.
- **Support for educational teams** – a shared language between home, preschool and therapy, so progress continues everywhere.
